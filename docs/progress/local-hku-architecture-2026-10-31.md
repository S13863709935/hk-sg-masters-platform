## Programme and matching boundaries

Other unconsulted examination-format policies remain unresolved.

[Ordinary MArch](https://www.arch.hku.hk/programmes/arch/master-of-architecture/admissions/) requires an Architectural Studies degree or accepted equivalent; work experience is preferred, not essential, and no absolute GPA is set in its FAQ. The self-funded and UGC paths require university classification, not inference from a student's residence. [MArch (Design)](https://www.arch.hku.hk/programmes/arch/master-of-architecture-design/admissions/) is a distinct three-year August award accepting honours Science/Engineering/Arts degrees or accepted equivalent. These award types do not establish a fixed major list or honours-class threshold. Its current one-page supporting checklist makes a portfolio optional; the ordinary MArch portfolio is required.

[MLA](https://www.arch.hku.hk/programmes/dla/master-of-landscape-architecture/admissions/) retains named related backgrounds and an assessed exceptional-background alternative.
