## Conditions preserved

- LLM uses 24 credits without foundation courses or 30 with them, at HKD 8,600 per credit for the 2027/28 cohort. Arithmetic estimates are HKD 206,400 / 258,000 before separate charges. The school decides the applicable load. Academic entry has alternative legal qualifications; non-law experience exceptions and prior-qualification exclusions apply only to the named streams. The page says 2027 entry but retains August 2026 in an intensive-course note. This date conflict remains unresolved; no August 2027 date is fabricated.
- Arbitration uses 30 exempt or 33 non-exempt credits at HKD 7,600, giving conditional estimates of HKD 228,000 / 250,800. Qualification age and approved exemption remain manual. Prior dispute-resolution work is a preference. The CGPA 2.00 diploma-exit criterion is post-entry, not undergraduate admission GPA. Neither appointment as an arbitrator/mediator nor professional recognition follows automatically.
- Eight exact-load/mode applicant contexts retain law's different local and non-local deadlines. No category is selected by location, nationality or score. LLM local closes 30 June 2027 and arbitration local 31 May; both publish 30 April for non-local applicants. Dates come from their respective programme pages. Classification, credit-load applicability, rolling capacity and part-time permission need confirmation.
- Both law programmes publish conditional IELTS 6.5 / old TOEFL 88 / updated TOEFL 4.5. CET-6 520 with three-year validity stays manual. Neighbouring programmes' IELTS 6 / updated TOEFL 4 interview trigger is not copied into these higher-score law branches.
- Veterinary Medicine requires a recognised BVM, DVM or equivalent veterinary qualification. Public Health lists relevant fields and separate Research Training / Practicum / Global study streams; Global has a limited quota and possible interview. Neither degree establishes a professional licence or a guaranteed placement.
- Business and Data Analytics accepts any discipline only with appropriate mathematical preparation. IAM/QAB are alternative streams under P84, not three degrees because the directory repeats it under departments. Its 30-credit estimate uses HKD 12,000 per credit.
- BIS requires an explicit MIS or FIT selection. MIS's non-IT preference is not a hard IT-graduate ban. FIT's intermediate IT/IS or Finance/Accounting knowledge must be evidenced; neither a major label nor generic work years proves it. Each route retains its own body and fee source, even though both publish 30 credits at HKD 11,000 per credit and a shared 31 May 2027 deadline.

The [official central list](https://www.cb.cityu.edu.hk/en/pg/taught-postgraduate-programmes/list) supplies the 15 September 2026 opening and 15 September 2024 earliest TOEFL/IELTS test-date anchor. The [official FAQ](https://www.cb.cityu.edu.hk/en/pg/taught-postgraduate-programmes/faq) supplies the separate HKD 600 ordinary-choice application fee and single-test format policy. EMBA is expressly a HKD 1,000 exception; no ordinary-choice fee is attached to the unresolved EMBA parent.
