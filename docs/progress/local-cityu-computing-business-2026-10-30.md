AI for Sciences' non-local/non-English-medium applicability also remains manual. Source-specific CET-6 values, excluded test formats and language interview triggers are visible notes, not unsupported automatic comparisons. Where this partial extraction did not establish a CET-6 number, none is borrowed from another programme.

Subject relevance, alternative qualifications, professional membership, course preparation and interviews remain assessed where required. GPA is not invented from honours or graduation rules. Completing recorded comparisons still does not establish full eligibility, an admission probability or guaranteed admission.
