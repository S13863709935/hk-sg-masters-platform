## One master, alternative foundations and distinct modes

The [MEd page](https://www.suss.edu.sg/programmes/detail/master-of-education) and [international directory](https://www.suss.edu.sg/admissions/international-students/international-graduate-students) identify one coursework master, existing `SG-SUSS-P011`, under the Teaching & Learning Centre. Students combine one 30-CU foundation, GDED or GDCT, with the 30-CU GDLDT deepening component. Three available diplomas do not mean 90 required CU or three master degrees. Standalone diplomas are separate awards. Master-award CGPA 3.0 and the no-thesis structure are study criteria, not undergraduate entry GPA.

Regular MEDU is part-time for two to four years. Accelerated MEDU-A is full-time for one year and only uses GDED-GDLDT. Two study options record these modes/durations. Regular-mode selection does not identify or approve a foundation; programme-level manual conditions remain unresolved. No new verified route-evidence group is manufactured from mode selection.

Only GDCT-GDLDT has the stated two-year teaching/training-equivalent experience and employer practicum-capacity condition. A universal numeric work rule would wrongly exclude GDED applicants, including career switchers. No mandatory Education undergraduate major, numeric undergraduate GPA or honours classification was established. Generic experience years do not verify the relevance/equivalence or organisation's practicum capacity.

## International master versus standalone diplomas

International master applicants must select MEDU-A and the GDED specialisation, subject to programme criteria and a valid Student Pass. These facts are not inferred from residence, fees or academic scores.

The [GDED](https://www.suss.edu.sg/programmes/detail/graduate-diploma-in-education) and [GDLDT](https://www.suss.edu.sg/programmes/detail/graduate-diploma-in-learning-design-and-technology) pages formally restrict standalone admission to citizens, PRs or Singapore residents and direct international applicants to a master, while their FAQs also welcome international students. This ambiguity remains visible; it neither permits standalone admission nor overrides explicit master access. The separate [GDCT](https://www.suss.edu.sg/programmes/detail/graduate-diploma-in-curriculum-and-teaching) page has citizen/PR-only standalone scope, work-study delivery and workplace practicum requirements. These are not universal master conditions.

Regular lessons are on weekday evenings or Saturdays. Typical advice is 15–20 CU per semester, or 30 CU for accelerated/international students. GDCT's workplace asynchronous learning, paired practicum courses and mentoring do not promise a fully remote master or remove organisational capacity requirements. CET completion after candidature expiry does not restore award eligibility. No teacher registration or employment entitlement is inferred.
