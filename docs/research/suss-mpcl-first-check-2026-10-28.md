## Identity and admissions

The [programme page](https://www.suss.edu.sg/programmes/detail/master-of-psychology-clinical) and [international directory](https://www.suss.edu.sg/admissions/international-students/international-graduate-students) support one separate Clinical coursework master, existing `SG-SUSS-P007` / MPCL. Ordinary MPSY `SG-SUSS-P006`, its Organisational/Forensic tracks and smaller awards remain separate. A dissertation does not override the official coursework classification.

The usual route specifies a psychology/related honours degree at Second Upper and two years of full-time or equivalent psychology work. However, the same page permits no-honours/lower-classification applications subject to other requirements, holistic assessment and conditional PRCPSY. Therefore the data does not turn standard classification into an unconditional rejection rule. Exact Psychology is comparable; related degree titles remain unresolved. Work years can expose a numeric gap, but cannot verify relevance or full-time equivalence.

PRCPSY has 30 Level-4 CU and CGPA 3.0, is self-funded and awards no separate qualification. Bridge completion/approval is not assumed. Neither bridge nor master-award CGPA is undergraduate entry GPA, nor are preparatory units automatically counted as a 105-CU master. No credit exemptions are offered. Recognition/route assessment remains with SUSS.
