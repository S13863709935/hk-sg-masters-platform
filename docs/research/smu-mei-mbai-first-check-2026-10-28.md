## Matching and materials

Both accept a good bachelor in any field. The 1–5-year experience description expressly allows other candidates to be considered; it is neither a hard minimum nor a maximum. MBAI additionally welcomes motivated applicants without experience and states technical/quantitative background is helpful, with no coding requirement. Major names, total years and GPA do not verify individual fit or experience exceptions.

Recent-alumni CGPA substitutes for test evidence are institution/year-specific (SMU 3.0/4.0; named local autonomous alumni 3.5/5.0 within five graduation years), with tests potentially still requested. They are not universal admission floors or automatic funding. MBAI's AIQ alternatives are GMAT 600 / Focus 595 / GRE 312 / SMUAT 55; these are not cross-version conversions, a requirement to take every test, or MEI thresholds. No MEI numeric test minimum was established in its consulted admissions block. The profile lacks these entrance-test/institution/year details, so accepted evidence remains manual.

Both condition TOEFL/IELTS on undergraduate teaching not being English and state two-year validity from testing; the precise assessment point needs confirmation. MBAI labels IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 90 as reference benchmarks, not an unconditional rule for all candidates. MEI does not inherit them, and neither inherits MSA Band 4.5 or its submission-time anchor. High English scores or a self-reported waiver do not settle medium-of-instruction proof.

MEI's current two statements are approximately 500 words for motivation/classroom contribution and 300 words with creation/reflection and ethics subparts; actual form interpretation needs confirmation. MBAI's detailed list requires two 500–600-word statements, while a short summary says statement of purpose; retain the detailed list rather than assume one essay is enough. Resume, transcripts, identity, applicable test evidence and two referees are listed; MBAI FAQ also lists the bachelor certificate. Referee invitations follow actual university application; originals need matriculation verification and shortlisted applicants are interviewed. The platform does not write/collect/email/submit applicant content.
