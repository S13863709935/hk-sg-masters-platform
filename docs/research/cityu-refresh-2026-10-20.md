## Decisions and limits

Computer Science charges by credit and category. For the minimum 30 credits, `30 × 8,600 = HKD 258,000` locally and `30 × 10,100 = HKD 303,000` for non-local students. These are calculated minimum-credit estimates, not quoted fixed totals. No category is inferred; no uncollected application fee becomes zero. Both study modes keep explicit selection and immigration/combined-mode caveats.

Economics publishes HKD 354,000 for 2027; this agrees with 30 credits at the current HKD 11,800 rate. Separate costs remain unverified. The recorded opening day is 15 September 2026; closing dates remain programme-specific and rolling review can finish earlier.

The IELTS/TOEFL floor is explicitly 15 September 2024, inclusive, for 2027 admissions. It is not a rolling expiry from today's date. Test-version checks remain separate. English waivers, CET-6/equivalence and interviews still require confirmation. Neither programme is promoted to complete requirements, independently reviewed status or an admission tier.

Category evidence retains per-observation dates: the older EdUHK context remains 19 October. Cross-subdomain CityU sources are allowed only within the same allowlisted university domain; another university, spoofed host or stale offering cannot override a category.
