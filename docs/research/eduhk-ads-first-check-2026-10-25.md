## Decisions retained in the data

Use existing `HK-EDUHK-P044`, code A1M137, not a new catalogue entry. The programme is a one-year FT taught MSc, 24 credits. No PT or January cohort invented. Current 2027/28 fee plus central September 2027 schedule establish the recorded offering.

Science/engineering/relevant backgrounds are preferences, not an exclusive eligibility set. No hard major, GPA, honours or experience rule was created. Recognition, degree equivalence and the conditional shortlisted interview remain manual. Python is curriculum content, not a prerequisite certificate.

Teaching is Putonghua supported by English. The central entrance page separates English-medium from Chinese-medium admissions. Applying its English-medium score table to ADS would be unjustified; absence of a numeric score in the programme/leaflet is not an English exemption either. Generic FAQ/document English-proof wording leaves applicability unresolved. Keep language unknown and obtain programme-office confirmation; do not import MPPM's test scores, exclusions or validity dates.

Gross tuition is a whole-programme amount. Application fees and final cutoffs differ by EdUHK local/non-local status, so provide two explicit categories with no default. Keep early/main/final rounds separate; exact times and programme-capacity closure are not established. School classification is not inferred from Singapore residence or nationality. Acceptance payment credits tuition; no extra deposit amount, scholarship deduction, duration multiplier or all-in price was invented.

Document PDF branches distinguish final-year, alumni, Mainland-study qualifications and Mainland applicants with overseas qualifications. Do not impose all branches on every student. The additional-documents list does not name ADS; this does not prevent later requests. In particular its other programme's PSC certificate and portfolio requirements are not ADS gates. Referees/study statement stay conditional on programme/application instructions.
