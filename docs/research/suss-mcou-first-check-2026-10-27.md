## Entry and selection

Prior course recognition needs documentary assessment. Degree title, employment years and stackable diplomas cannot automatically establish exemption. Programme selection and course recognition remain manual. The [one-page applicant instructions](https://www.suss.edu.sg/docs/default-source/content_programmes/imported/content/instructions-to-applicants-for-personal-statement-and-referral-letters-pdf.pdf?sfvrsn=6ab8ca43_4) allow family/friend referee alternatives for applicants not employed; do not invent an employment minimum. The platform does not collect statements, psychological-test results, referee letters or sensitive life histories.

International applicants are explicitly included, subject to Student Pass arrangements; MGER's resident restriction is not inherited.
