## Why these two

Sources: [current programme catalogue](https://prog-crs.hkust.edu.hk/pgprog/2027-28/pgd-msc-ciem) and [school programme page](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/academics/taught-postgraduate/msc-ciem).

Seven concentrations remain curriculum choices within one degree. A project component does not make the taught programme a research-entry degree. Professional-recognition wording for intake 2026 is not promoted to a 2027 guarantee.

Create three explicit study options under the existing ID. February 2027 is part-time only, HKD 180,000, with a common local/non-local 1 November 2026 cutoff. The school's early-September opening phrase is not an exact opening date. September 2027 FT/PT totals are HKD 210,000 / 180,000. Instalments and fee-covered terms are different from multiplying an annual fee. Application fee HKD 600 is separate; extra/retaken courses or additional time can cost more.

Two September contexts bind four applicant options. First round is 15 December 2026; final round is 1 April 2027 non-local or 15 May 2027 local. No exact opening/time, default category or visa judgement is inferred. February does not inherit September rounds. Ordinary credit transfer and PGD-to-MSc progression still pay full MSc tuition; the restricted integrated pathway has separate pro-rata rules.

Record second-class honours / Civil Engineering or related engineering and explicit IELTS/iBT thresholds. Recognition, relatedness, remedial study, legacy test formats, waiver evidence and validity remain manual. No numeric general admission GPA is invented.
