## Background and selection evidence

The degree is MSA, distinct from MPA, with Data & Analytics and Applied Artificial Intelligence tracks within one master. Recognition/good undergraduate performance and sufficient background require assessment. Main admissions mentions Mathematics/Calculus; header/FAQ mentions Accounting/Finance/Statistics/Business. FAQ evidence alternatives include GMAT quantitative percentile, satisfactory university mathematics, two polytechnic maths courses at minimum B, or acceptable GCE maths/equivalent. This is not a strict undergraduate-major list or a requirement to satisfy every alternative. A major or high overall GPA alone cannot prove sufficient preparation.

Relevant work is advantageous, not compulsory; historical cohort averages do not establish years required. Recent-alumni GPA substitutes for test evidence are institution/year-specific: SMU 3.0/4.0 or named local autonomous graduates 3.5/5.0, within five graduation years, subject to SMU requesting a test. These and separate scholarship GPAs are not universal admission floors. GMAT/GRE/SMUAT alternatives remain manual. The main page frames GMAT 600 / Focus 595 / GRE combined 312 as competitiveness guidance while FAQ calls them minimums; do not turn that into a universal GMAT gate or convert score versions. GMAT/GRE validity is five years before application submission.

For non-English undergraduate instruction, the page explicitly publishes TOEFL 90 or Band 4.5, or IELTS 6.5, and two-year validity before application submission. Both TOEFL values are university-published alternatives, not a platform conversion. Language-medium proof, version and FAQ undergraduate/graduate wording need assessment, so no automatic high-score or self-reported waiver approval is introduced. The current profile does not capture the actual submission date or accepted teaching-language evidence.

Documents include degree/transcript, resume, passport, applicable tests, additional supporting evidence and two referees. The listed referee relationships include colleagues, superiors or friends; no universal academic/work pair is invented. FAQ also calls for a career-fit statement without an established word limit; MAF's two 500–600-word statements are not inherited. Shortlisted applicants have an interview. University referee invitations follow actual application; the platform does not collect/write/send/submit materials. A professional or higher qualification without a bachelor is expressly rejected in the consulted FAQ; its accreditation wording does not independently establish professional status.
