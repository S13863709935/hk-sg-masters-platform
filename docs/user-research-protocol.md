# User and service research protocol

Prepared on 13 October 2026 as an internal product-research procedure. No interviews, survey responses or findings are reported here.

## Purpose

Understand how students currently find Hong Kong and Singapore taught master's information, which details are difficult to compare, and whether source-linked requirement explanations are understandable. This is information support, not application handling or an admission prediction service.

## Planned method

1. Ask volunteers for consent before a short exploratory conversation. A first round of approximately 4–6 students is a planning target, not a completed sample.
2. Discuss their information-search steps, comparison difficulties, preferred programme details and interpretation of unknown requirements.
3. Use synthetic backgrounds and programme examples for any prototype task. Do not request real transcripts, passport details, CVs, contact lists or named GPA records for the repository.
4. Record anonymous observations, task obstacles and suggested improvements. Separate observations from the team's interpretation.
5. Summarise the actual sample, limitations and resulting requirement changes only after the activities happen.

## Service comparison

Review university directories and any relevant information-aggregation services using the same checklist: coverage boundary, source traceability, applicable intake, comparison fields, treatment of missing information, update transparency and presence of application/probability claims. Record the URL and actual access date; do not write unsupported competitor findings.

## Planned outputs

An anonymous observation summary, service-comparison notes and a prioritised list of requirement changes. These outputs remain pending until real research is conducted and documented.
